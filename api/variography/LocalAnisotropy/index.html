    LocalAnisotropy ¶

    Orientation (azimuth, dip, rake in degrees), range ratios (semi/major, minor/major) and range scales at a set of locations.

    A scale multiplies every range of the variogram it is used with, and the search radius, at that location; scales defaults to 1, a constant or one per location.

    at method descriptor ¶

    at(targets)

    The anisotropy of the nearest location, at targets.

    from_grid staticmethod ¶

    from_grid(model, column, *, window=2, ratios=None)

    From the gradient of a block-model attribute: the structure tensor summed over window cells each side; the least-change direction is the major axis. Without ratios, ratios follow the tensor's eigenvalues.

    from_mesh staticmethod ¶

    from_mesh(mesh, targets, *, major='dip', ratios=Ellipsis)

    At targets, from the nearest triangle of mesh: the normal is the minor axis and major is "dip" or "strike".

    from_parquet staticmethod ¶

    from_parquet(path)

    Reads to_parquet output; raises InvalidInput on another class's file or a newer format.

    from_points staticmethod ¶

    from_points(coords, *, k=20, ratios=None)

    From a point cloud: principal axes of each point's k nearest points.

    smooth method descriptor ¶

    smooth(radius)

    Averages orientation tensors, ratios and scales within radius of each location.

    to_parquet method descriptor ¶

    to_parquet(path)

    Writes locations, angles and ratios as Parquet columns; from_parquet reads them back.
