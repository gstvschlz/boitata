*[collar]: The point where a drill hole starts at the surface, with its coordinates and total length.
*[desurveying]: Turning a hole's collar and downhole survey angles into 3D coordinates along the hole.
*[swath plot]: Mean grade of blocks and samples in parallel slices along one direction, to find local bias.
*[cutoff]: The grade below which a block is not worth mining; resources are reported above it.
*[grade–tonnage curve]: Tonnes and mean grade above each cutoff, read as a curve over the cutoffs.
*[contained metal]: Tonnes × mean grade above a cutoff: the amount of metal the resource holds.
*[smoothing]: The loss of variance in an estimate, which averages neighboring samples and so varies less than the true grades.
