*[support]: the volume a value represents: a point, a length of core or a block.
*[composite]: a drill-hole interval of fixed length whose grade is the sampled-length-weighted mean of the assays it covers.
*[composites]: drill-hole intervals of fixed length whose grades are the sampled-length-weighted means of the assays they cover.
*[volume-variance effect]: averaging values over larger supports keeps their mean and reduces their variance.
*[preferential sampling]: sampling that places more samples in some zones, usually the rich ones, than in others.
*[declustering]: weighting samples by the area or volume each represents, to correct statistics for clustered sampling.
*[top cut]: a cap: values above it are set to it, to limit the influence of a few extreme values.
*[domain]: a volume of rock whose values you treat as one population, such as one lithology or one vein.
*[coefficient of variation]: the standard deviation divided by the mean, a unitless measure of spread.
*[skewness]: a measure of the asymmetry of a distribution; positive when a long tail runs to high values.
*[outlier]: a value far from the rest of the distribution, often real but with outsized influence.
